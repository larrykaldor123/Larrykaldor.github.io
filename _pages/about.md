# Hi, I'm Can.

I am a Master of Applied Accounting student at the Australian National University, with an academic background in accounting and a strong interest in audit, finance, data analytics, and AI-assisted programming.

My experience spans accounting and audit internships, financial data analysis, and data-driven projects. I enjoy combining accounting knowledge with tools such as Excel, Power Query, Python, SQL, and AI to turn complex financial data into structured and actionable insights.

I am particularly interested in how technology can improve traditional accounting, auditing, and financial analysis workflows.
