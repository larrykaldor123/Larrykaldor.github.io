## Project Overview

This project focused on evaluating potential overseas markets for Chinese smart lock products through data-driven analysis.

The objective was to integrate international trade and macroeconomic data, clean and standardize the datasets, and develop a quantitative scoring framework to compare potential target markets.

The project combined business analysis, data processing, Python programming, and interactive visualization to support market selection with quantitative evidence.

## Data Collection

The project integrated data from multiple international trade and macroeconomic sources.

The raw datasets contained information covering different countries, years, and economic and trade indicators. Before analysis, the datasets were cleaned and structured to ensure consistency across companies, countries, and financial years.

## Data Cleaning & Processing

**Power Query** was used as the primary tool for data cleaning and transformation.

The workflow included:

- Cleaning and standardizing raw datasets
- Standardizing company and country identifiers
- Aligning financial years across datasets
- Combining multiple datasets using common identifiers
- Reshaping and transforming data for analysis
- Checking data completeness and consistency
- Removing incomplete observations according to the project criteria

After cleaning and integration, a structured dataset was created for further analysis.

## Indicator Selection

The analysis selected **12 core indicators** to evaluate different dimensions of overseas market attractiveness.

The indicators covered areas including:

- Market size
- GDP growth
- Import demand
- Export activity
- Trade performance
- Economic conditions
- Market development potential

The selected indicators were designed to provide a comprehensive view of the attractiveness of different potential markets rather than relying on a single metric.

## Python Data Processing

After the initial cleaning and integration in Power Query, **Python and pandas** were used for further data processing and analysis.

Python was used to:

- Process the cleaned dataset
- Calculate and organize analytical indicators
- Transform data into an analysis-ready format
- Perform indicator standardization
- Generate data required for scoring and visualization

This workflow made repetitive calculations more efficient and provided a reproducible approach to the analysis.

## Standardization

Because the 12 indicators were measured using different units and scales, the raw values could not be directly compared.

A **Min-Max Standardization** approach was applied to transform the indicators onto a common scale.

The standardized results were then converted into a **0–10 scoring framework**, allowing different indicators to be compared within the same analytical system.

## Market Scoring & Comparison

The standardized indicator scores were combined to evaluate the overall attractiveness of different potential markets.

The scoring framework allowed each market to be compared across the 12 indicators and provided an overall basis for market ranking and selection.

This approach helped transform a large amount of fragmented international data into a structured decision-making framework.

## Data Visualization

The analytical results were visualized to make differences between potential markets easier to understand.

The project included:

- Comparative indicator visualizations
- Market scoring visualizations
- **Radar charts** showing the performance of selected markets across multiple indicators
- Interactive **HTML-based visualizations**

The visualization layer allowed users to explore market characteristics and compare different countries more intuitively.
