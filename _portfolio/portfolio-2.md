## Project Overview

This project explored how data analytics can be applied to audit risk identification in the pharmaceutical industry.

As regulatory requirements become increasingly stringent and financial data becomes more complex, traditional audit procedures may face challenges in efficiently identifying potential risks within the sales cycle.

The project aimed to develop a systematic, data-driven audit analytics framework to improve the efficiency and precision of risk identification and provide quantitative support for audit decision-making.

---

## Data Preparation

The project used **Python, Excel, and AI-assisted tools** to process financial data from listed pharmaceutical companies.

Data corresponding to **29 categories of financial indicators** was cleaned, filtered, and standardized.

The datasets were integrated using:

**Stock Code + Annual Period**

as the matching key.

The resulting standardized dataset covered:

- **8 years** of financial data
- **29 financial indicators**
- **31 columns**

This standardized dataset provided a consistent foundation for subsequent audit analytics.

---

## Financial Indicator Analysis

From the standardized dataset, **6 core financial indicators** were selected for further analysis.

These indicators were standardized to enable comparisons across companies and financial periods.

The analysis focused on identifying abnormal financial characteristics that could potentially indicate risks within the sales cycle.

---

## Comparable Company Identification

A **two-stage K-means clustering approach** was applied to identify companies with similar financial characteristics.

The clustering process grouped companies based on their financial features and helped establish a more appropriate comparable-company framework for subsequent risk assessment.

This approach reduced reliance on simple industry-wide averages and allowed companies to be evaluated against financially comparable peers.

---

## Multi-Dimensional Risk Assessment

Risk assessment was conducted from three complementary perspectives:

1. **Historical trends of the company**
2. **Performance of comparable companies**
3. **Industry averages**

Difference thresholds of **20%, 30%, and 50%** were established to implement a hierarchical risk-warning mechanism.

Different levels of deviation could therefore generate corresponding risk signals and help prioritize areas requiring further audit investigation.

---

## High-Risk Revenue Analysis

For revenue items identified as potentially high risk, further substantive audit analysis was performed.

The analysis focused on:

- **Unit prices**
- **Discounts**
- **Logistics information**
- **Abnormal transaction patterns**

These additional procedures were designed to investigate whether unusual financial patterns could be supported by underlying business activities.

---

## AI-Assisted Analysis

AI-assisted tools were incorporated into the project to support data analysis and risk identification.

AI was used to assist with:

- Data-processing logic
- Python programming
- Financial data analysis
- Identification of potential abnormal transaction patterns
- Analysis of equity relationships
- Exploration of relationships between related parties and unusual transactions

AI was used as an analytical assistant, while the audit objectives, risk assessment framework, and interpretation of potential audit risks were defined based on the project requirements.

## Project Outcome

The project developed a **pharmaceutical industry sales-cycle audit risk assessment model** capable of identifying and warning against risk signals from multiple dimensions and at multiple levels.

The framework provided a more systematic approach to screening potential audit risks and demonstrated how financial data analytics can complement traditional audit procedures.
